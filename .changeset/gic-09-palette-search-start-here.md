The graph explorer gets search, an entry state and a minimap (GIC-09, #670, epic #661). `ExplorerCommandPalette` opens with Ctrl/Cmd+K, `/` or the new search field in the top bar: On canvas (local fuzzy match over the loaded labels; flies the camera there and selects it), In your graph (`GET /graph/entities?q=`; Enter adds a seed, Alt+Enter replaces the seeds, capped at 10 with a message) and Actions (Expand selection, Hide selection, Re-layout, Switch to list); `type:Person joe` tokens narrow the results; ARIA combobox and listbox, a live region for the result count, Escape returns focus to the canvas; a full-screen sheet with 44px rows on a compact window, a 640px modal otherwise. "Connect A and B" and the Lenses are not offered until those capabilities exist. "Start here" replaces the bare seed search when there is nothing to open (and on Home, `?start=1`): Themes from the overview clusters (seeding through the existing overview hand-off, with the building, stale and too-large notices), "Who and what is active" (the most-mentioned People, Projects, Products and Topics of the last 30 days) and Recent, in a 1, 2 or 3 column grid. `ExplorerMinimap` draws the node positions and the camera viewport as an `aria-hidden` SVG; dragging pans. It is never shown on a compact window, optional on a medium one and on above 150 nodes on an expanded one (`minimapVisible`). `GraphCanvasControls` gains `focusNode`, `getViewport`, `panToGraph` and `onFrame` (all optional) and the type `CanvasViewport`. API: `GET /graph/entities` accepts `sort=mentions` and `since=<YYYY-MM-DD or ISO datetime>`: the top `limit` entities by the notes and transcripts that mention them since `since`, owner-only, no further pages (additive: `listEntitiesQuerySchema`, `GraphEntityListParams.since`).
