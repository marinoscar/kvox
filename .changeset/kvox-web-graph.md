Add `@marinoscar/kvox-web/graph/headless`, `/graph/ui` and `/graph/testing`. The graph slice moves the connected-knowledge client (`/api/graph/**` and the `/api/ai-tasks/config` read), the entity, brief, timeline, mention, evidence, proposal, ontology, attribute-definition, preference, overview, export, import and extract-estimate hooks, the explorer and overview models, and the index, entity, explorer, overview, import and Knowledge graph settings pages into the package, over `KvoxWebHost` only (the preferences hook reads and writes `/api/user-settings` through the host with `If-Match` and one retry on a 409). The `knowledge` destination (gated on `graph:read` and the `graph` feature, owning `/graph` and `/ask`), its six routes and the Knowledge graph card are data (`kvoxGraphDestinations`, `kvoxGraphDestinationRoutes`, `kvoxGraphRoutes`, `kvoxGraphUserSettingsSections`). Ready-made slot components fill the transcript and note pages and both libraries (`graphTranscriptPageSlots`, `graphNotePageSlots`, `GraphSearchPrefix`), each returning nothing unless `host.features().graphEnabled` is on and the viewer holds the permission. The slice imports only `core`, `transcripts`, `notes` and `search`, never `ask`: the entity page takes the Ask action and panel as slots (`GraphEntityPageSlots`). `sigma`, `@react-sigma/core`, `graphology` and `graphology-layout-forceatlas2` are optional peers of `graph/ui` only; `GraphCanvas.tsx` is the one file that imports sigma and loads as a lazy chunk. `graph/testing` exports `mockGraphCanvas` for a host's tests, and `core/testing`'s fake host passes the caller's abort `signal` to a route.
