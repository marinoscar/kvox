Add `@marinoscar/kvox-web/notes/headless` and `/notes/ui`. The notes slice moves the four clients (`/api/notes/**`, `/api/note-templates/**`, note exports and the generation stream), the note, template, generation-context and template-preview hooks, and the library, new-note, note, history and note-template pages into the package, over `KvoxWebHost` only. The generation stream (`connectNoteStream`, `connectNoteGenerationStream`) runs over `host.sse` with the core reconnect loop and resumes with `Last-Event-ID`; every delta is still written at its offset, so a replay duplicates nothing. The `notes` destination, its routes (the four `/notes` paths and `/settings/note-templates`) and the Note Templates card are data (`kvoxNotesDestinations`, `kvoxNotesDestinationRoutes`, `kvoxNotesRoutes`, `kvoxNotesUserSettingsSections`). The slice imports only `core`, `transcripts` and `search`: the knowledge-graph features of the note page are slots the app fills (`NotePageSlots`: `Provider`, `useAsideWidth`, `HeaderActions`, `ProposalActions`, `SelectionActions`), as is the search-hits prefix of the library, each contained so a slot that throws renders nothing; the viewer's AI key and model list come in as the `ai` adapter (`NotesAiAccess`). `react-markdown`, `remark-gfm` and `@tiptap/*` are optional peers of `notes/ui` only, and the visual editor is loaded lazily. `core/ui` gains `KvoxCopyButton`.
