Add `@marinoscar/kvox-web/transcripts/headless` and `/transcripts/ui`. The transcripts slice moves the six clients of `/api/transcripts/**` (reads, edits, versions, exports, name checks and the resumable upload sessions), the transcript, version, word, search, name-check, playback and source-audio hooks, the upload manager (`KvoxUploadManagerProvider`, mounted once at the app root) and the library, viewer, history and new-transcript pages into the package, over `KvoxWebHost` only, with the `transcripts` destination and the four routes as data (`kvoxTranscriptsDestinations`, `kvoxTranscriptsDestinationRoutes`, `kvoxTranscriptsRoutes`). The slice imports only `core`, `transcription` and `search`: the notes section, the speaker person links, the selection actions and the header actions of `TranscriptPage`, and the search-hits prefix of the library, are slots the app fills, each contained so a slot that throws renders nothing; sharing and AI availability come in as adapters. `KvoxWebHost` gains the optional `useLatestEventId` hook (a notification-stream fast path for list polling) and `KvoxRouteDef` gains `redirectTo`. `@tanstack/react-virtual` is an optional peer of `transcripts/ui`.
