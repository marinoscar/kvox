The graph explorer gains path mode, "Connect A and B" (GIC-13, #674, epic #661). A picker (two entity fields searched like the seed search, a swap button, the longest connection from 2 to 5 hops, relation-type chips) asks `GET /graph/paths` for up to three ranked paths, and `PathStrip` reads each as a story with receipts: node chips joined by edge connectors (relation, role, stage and stance chips, the number of sources) with the top quote of each edge under it, its source and date as a link, and "N more" opening that edge in the inspector on its Evidence tab. A segmented control switches between Path 1, 2 and 3, labelled "strong", "moderate" or "weak evidence" from the API's score. "Show on canvas" merges the chosen path into the graph under the node cap, highlights it (the canvas dims every other node and edge through new `pathNodeIds` and `pathEdgeIds` props of `GraphCanvas`) and fits the camera to it (new `fitTo` on `GraphCanvasControls`). The strip is vertical inside the bottom sheet on a phone (with a full-screen picker), horizontal with scroll-snap in a canvas overlay on a tablet, and the same overlay docked and collapsible on a wide window. Entry points: the top bar's Connect button, "Path to…" in the inspector (A pre-filled, next to the host's `NodeActions`), the node context menu, and the URL, `?pathFrom=&pathTo=` (with `pathHops` and `pathRel`), which is shareable and restored on reload; `?pathFrom=` alone opens the picker with A filled in. An empty answer reads "No connection within N hops" with buttons to look one hop further or drop the relation filters, and a 503 reads "That search took too long, try fewer hops". A hop that touches a person fact never asks for evidence. Each hop is a button named by a sentence ("Joe Rivera, involved in as stakeholder, skeptical, Copilot rollout") and the list is named by the whole path. `useGraphExplorer` gains `mergeExternalSlice`. "Explain this path" is not offered yet: the host's Ask slot takes an entity, not scoped evidence.
