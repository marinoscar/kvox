Add `@marinoscar/kvox-api/graph`, `/graph-extraction` and `/graph-rdf`, each with a `/testing` entry: `GraphModule.forRoot` (the single graph write path, the read layer, the access rules, the ontology and attribute services, the entity brief, the overview layout, the speaker link, `kg.migrate` and `kg.purge`), `GraphExtractionModule.forRoot` (`kg.extract`, `kg.resolve`, `kg.embed`, the proposal stages and the review-and-commit routes) and `GraphRdfModule.forRoot` (the OWL and SHACL artefacts, `kg.export`, `kg.import`) over the host ports and the transcripts, notes and search slices, with `describeGraphConformance`, `describeGraphExtractionConformance`, `describeGraphRdfConformance` and `GRAPH_PERMISSIONS`. The extraction subscribes to `kvox.notes.note_ready` on `KVOX_EVENTS` itself, so the notes slice never knows the graph exists. The ports gain, additively, `KVOX_JOBS.findLatest` and `runNow`, `KVOX_OBJECT_STORE.listManaged`, the `maxWait` transaction option, and, on `KVOX_AI`, the context `jobId`, the strict `jsonSchema` and `maxOutputTokens` of `generateStructured`, the error classes `truncated` and `invalid_output` and `KvoxAiError.usage`. `search` now exports `SEARCH_TRANSCRIPT_VISIBILITY` from its module. `graphology` (four packages), `n3`, `jsonld` and `rdf-validate-shacl` join the optional peers.
