"What connects these notes" (GIC-14, #675, epic #661). `POST /api/graph/connect` (`graph:read`, owner-only, one 404 for any unreadable input) takes `noteIds` and `transcriptIds` (up to 10 each), `entityIds` (up to 20), `as_of` and `maxHops` (1 to 3) and answers `{ inputs, shared, paths, slice, truncated }`: the entities touched by two or more inputs ranked by input coverage then mention count (a document touches the entities of its readable `kg_mentions`; an entity touches itself and its one-hop neighbours), the bridge between each pair of inputs from `GraphPathsService` inside one 3 s budget, and the slice that draws them. A `sensitive` PersonFact never appears. At least two distinct inputs, or a single note or transcript for "what this note touches". `@marinoscar/kvox-contract` adds `graphConnectRequestSchema` and `graphConnectResponseSchema` (with their types and `GRAPH_CONNECT_MAX_*`); `@marinoscar/kvox-api` adds `GraphConnectService` (exported from `/graph`); `@marinoscar/kvox-web` adds `postGraphConnect()`. Explorer: Shift-click toggles a node in the selection, a lasso (Shift+drag on the stage, or a plain drag with the Select tool, now offered to a pointer too) marks the nodes inside it (pure geometry in `lassoGeometry.ts`), Space marks the focused node in Select mode, and touch keeps Select mode taps; the Selection view gains "Find connections", whose answer shows "3 people, 2 products and 1 risk in common", the shared entities (ringed on the canvas) and the bridges, in the inspector pane, overlay or bottom sheet by window class. `GraphCanvas` takes `selectTool` and `onLasso`, and `onNodeClick` receives `{ shiftKey }`; `useGraphExplorer` returns `addSlice` (a connect result is an Undo step like any other graph change); `ExplorerInspector` takes `connectResults`. `?connectNotes=id1,id2` restores the view and seeds the canvas from the answer. Notes: `NotesLibrarySlots` gains `useSelectionEnabled`, `SelectionActions` and `selectionLimit` (multi-select with checkboxes and a long press on touch), and `graphNotesLibrarySlots` fills them with "Explore connections" for 2 to 10 notes; the graph's note page header gains "Explore in graph" and "Compare with…" (a note picker). No host change is needed.
