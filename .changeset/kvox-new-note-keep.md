New note → "A document" now asks what to do with the chosen file: "Keep it as my note" (the default) imports the document itself as the note through an ad-hoc import batch, with inline progress, and opens it; "Rewrite it with a template" keeps the AI generation flow. The chooser accepts Word (.docx) and PowerPoint (.pptx) as well as PDF, Markdown and text, and a kept document's provenance reads "Imported from" its file.
