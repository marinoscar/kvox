New entry point `@marinoscar/kvox-ai/voiceprints`: on-device speaker voiceprints (CAM++ 512-wide, Apache-2.0) with a pinned model download, a Kaldi fbank, a lazily loaded `onnxruntime-web` embedder and a worker-thread wrapper, and pure window, similarity and suggestion policy helpers.
