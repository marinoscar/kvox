The graph explorer has a visual language (#667, epic #661). A node's type is its colour AND its bundled pictogram (a fixed colour-blind-safe palette of 14, light and dark, checked for 3:1 contrast and deuteranopia distinctness; explicit entries for `Topic`, `Product` and `Risk` ahead of #662 to #664, a deterministic fallback for any other type), its size is `√mentionCount` (else `√degree`), and seeds, pinned and selected nodes carry rings. Parallel edges are drawn as curves, edge width follows `evidenceCount` (else `confidence`), and an `INVOLVED_IN` edge takes its stance accent. Hover (the first tap on a touch screen) lights the node, its neighbours and their edges and dims the rest, through reducers rather than graph attributes. A degree-of-interest score ranks truncation and the keyboard order, and a first open shows the best 30 neighbours with a "+N more" stub per type that expands through the `types` filter. New `GraphLegend` (toggle buttons with `aria-pressed` and counts), `useLegendTypeFilter` (the `types` URL parameter) and `legendModel`. Re-enabling a filter now refetches what it removed. `@sigma/node-image`, `@sigma/node-border` and `@sigma/edge-curve` are new optional peers of `graph/ui` only (sigma 3.0.x compatible); the host application lists them.
